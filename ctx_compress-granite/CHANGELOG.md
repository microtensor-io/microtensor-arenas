# Changelog

- New arena, named after its base model Granite 4.0 H Micro. It succeeds ctx_compress, which closed after epoch 0, and its first epoch is its own epoch 0. Same task, grader, envelope, probe, profile and serving price as ctx_compress, with the runner, sandbox and replayer built from subnet 09d7b3f. Two pools on testnet and mainnet: the specialist model pool takes 60% and the full system pool 40% of all miner emission, with no serving or compute share, and an empty pool burns. The harness, router and escalation repair have no pools of their own and are scored inside the full system. A job runs 50 specialist model items and 50 system items and a pool is paid from 50 items. Its hidden items were never served in ctx_compress's epoch 0, and no item is served by both arenas. Submissions are free: the fee is 0 TAO.
